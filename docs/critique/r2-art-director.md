# Round 2 — Art Director critique (STATIC only)

Reviewer: `art-director`. Source: rendered PNGs in `.screenshots/r2b/` (1440 and 375, fold and full). No code was read except to locate selectors for fixes.
Capture caveats (not scored as defects): the pinned Journey leaves a blank pin-spacer in the full-page PNGs; the Requirement-to-Repeat cards were caught mid-reveal; the full-page PNG for `home` repeats the hero at the end and shows the fixed nav mid-page (stitching). Scoring: sub-scores are /5 (Composition, Brand, Type, A11y). Score is /10. Motion is not reviewed (n/a). Tags: CRITICAL / MAJOR / MINOR.

## Verdict

The site has a real point of view. The drawing-sheet heroes, the folded-A on About, the notched cards, the parts schedule and the burgundy footer could only belong to Alok. It is also repetitive. The same hero layout appears nine times, the same 3-degree "fold edge" separates every section, the same stats appear three times, and nine of sixteen catalogue cards are hollow text tiles. It is a strong 7.0, not an Awwwards 8.5. The gap to close is variety, scale contrast and a handful of real defects.

Site-wide average: **7.0 / 10** (47 sections).

## Site-wide findings

CRITICAL
1. Logo below the §2.6 minimum width (120px full lockup): about 93px in the nav, about 88px in the footer. Brand-lock violation. Fix in `Header.tsx` / `glass-nav.css` and `Footer.tsx`.
2. Text clipping: the Industries hero caption "PACKAGING & SPECIALIZED A…" (`IndustriesHeroArt.tsx` `.ih__cap`) and the product-card summary line-clamp (`products.css:70`) both truncate real copy.

MAJOR (ordered by priority)
1. Gradient text tails: every gradient headline word (`big machines`, `possibilities.`, `it from there.`, `keep coming back`) ends in about 2.2:1 pale rose. Clamp the gradient to 0–70%.
2. Hero 1440: the H1 crosses the diagonal onto the schematic, and about 250px of dead space sits above the eyebrow.
3. Pan-Bharat map: India is a speck, the stray "!" mark, no visible dispatch beyond India, a third tagline repetition.
4. Home "Why choose" is a three-icon equal feature row (§3.2 anti-pattern) that repeats the Proof strip, and its hairlines run through text.
5. Nine of sixteen catalogue cards are hollow text tiles; the Sealing single card is stretched to about 630px.
6. The same bottom-left hero composition on all nine inner pages; the top-left quadrant is empty on every one.
7. The same stats three times (home Proof, home Why choose, About In numbers), in inconsistent treatments.
8. Duplicate CTAs: the Enquiry band and the footer CTA stack on every page; the Career page has three.
9. Micro-type below the floor: 10–11px mono/caps in Industries captions, Parts schedule, two-path labels and contact schematic; card descriptions at 14px.
10. Generic icons (Requirement to Repeat, Why choose) read as SaaS; replace with the part pictograms.
11. The "FoldEdge" is the identical 3° line with a burgundy segment between every section, so the rhythm is monotonous and does not echo the 44° A-angle. Vary: one real 44° cut per page, one stepped (L+O) interlock, one square-ended.
12. The About Core values layout (empty right half, floating captions, first value caption missing).
13. Contact "Find us" is an empty box repeating the address a fourth time.
14. The Part detail hero repeats the gallery's pictogram, and the sticky bar is off-grid.
15. Career "Supportive" outline-only word and the "PEOPLE & CRAFT" ghost are illegible.
16. The Get a Quote glass button is too washed for the brand's one action colour.

MINOR: orphan hairline on About Story; the "APPLICATION" filler label; spelling consistency (recognized / recognised); FAQ as six identical boxes; Privacy pictogram.

## What is EXCELLENT and MUST BE PROTECTED

- The About hero folded-A with fan lines and the outline आलोक. It is the clearest translation of the logo into a page image.
- The drawing-sheet heroes (Products, Group, Contact, Enquiry): title blocks, leader lines, dimension arrows, "NOT TO SCALE". This is the site's signature, and Enquiry's labelled callouts are functional as well as decorative.
- The Parts schedule strip on the home page (a true engineering "schedule" that only Alok could have).
- Gradient numerals with measurement end-caps on the Proof strip; the Trust quote's burgundy/silver slashes.
- Notched/cut-corner cards (44° corner on product groups, buttons, tiles) and the staggered interlock of the product-group grid.
- The About white-to-burgundy diagonal split (Vision / Mission) and the Industries core-market burgundy slab. Both honour "one burgundy block per viewport".
- The burgundy footer with the large two-tone Devanagari line (white plus rose-pale) and the English line beneath.
- The nav active state (diamond plus underline) and the rising ↗ stair-step process on Industries.
- The honest empty states ("PRODUCT PHOTO COMING SOON", "Text to be published", "No open roles right now").
