## 6. Suggestions (abhi implement NAHI kiye, decision chahiye)

### 6.1 Content / flow

1. **Home "Two ways to start"** — sabse kamzor keep. Reason sirf "client ne maanga". Browse card upar ke product section ka repeat hai, custom card product group #5 jaisa hai. Option: sirf custom card rakhna, ya product groups ke andar merge.
2. **Home Industries bento** — `/industries/` ka full repeat (7 cards). Option: Home par chhota teaser (core market + link), poora bento sirf Industries page par.
3. **Trust ledger heading** ("We don't just mould plastic. We mould possibilities.") — slogan-type hai. Ledger rows heading se zyada kaam ki hain. Client ki core value hai, isliye hataya nahi, par About ke Core values me le jaana better rahega (#39).
4. **"We don't…" x3** — How We Work lead, CEO quote, Trust heading: teen jagah same sentence pattern. Locked copy hai, client se baat karke ek ko badalna chahiye.
5. **Part detail page par 3 CTAs** (Add to cart, Get a Quote, bottom "Need Float Valve?") — B2B ke liye ek primary action hona chahiye. Cart vs Quote ka flow clear karna.
6. **Group page "Sort by Price"** — saare prices "on request" hain, to price sort ka koi matlab nahi. Hata dena ya prices aane tak hide.
7. **About Story** — 5 lambe paragraphs, para 1 H2 ko repeat karta hai. Locked copy hai. Client approve kare to 4 paragraph me tight karna (draft meri taraf se ready hai: section 4).
8. **Career culture paragraph 2** — mobile par bahut lamba. Locked.
9. **Contact page** — phone/email/GSTIN `null` hain, to "Where to find us" me sirf address hai. Jaise hi client data de, bharna.
10. **Group names ki casing** — "Water Cooler spare parts", "Caster wheel", "On demand Customized Products": inconsistent. Client wording hai aur admin PHP seed me bhi hai, to ek saath badalna padega.
11. **Spelling** — "recognized" (vision, client) vs "recognised" (map legend) vs British "moulded". Client se confirm karke ek spelling.

### 6.2 Honesty / risk

- **"Quote within 24 hours"** (Develop step) — `journey.ts` me TODO(client) hai. Confirm hone tak live nahi jaana chahiye.
- **Journey 5 Cr+ / 12 Cr+** figures — owner ne diye, par TODO(client: confirm).
- **Privacy / Terms / Refund** — abhi "This page is being finalised" outline hain. Launch se pehle legal text chahiye.
- **WhatsApp number** — `null`. FAB tab tak kaam ka nahi.

### 6.3 SEO / technical

- **`check:seo` fail:** titles 65 chars se zyada, descriptions 160 se zyada (5 pages). Metadata chhota karna hoga (`src/lib/seo.ts` aur page metadata). Alag task.
- **Playwright 3 failures:** cart test text, career runtime test ka selector, aur home axe test ka timeout. Inhe baseline par chala ke confirm karna ki mere change se nahi aaye.
- **Highlight cap exception** (About story ke 4 marks): ya to rakhna (client bold), ya sirf 1 rakhna.

16 routes ka rendered DOM scan kiya (text + CSS pseudo-elements). Address hyphen ke alawa koi visible dash nahi mila.

### 8.2 Highlighter rebuild (`src/styles/ui.css`)

- **Problem:** purana band `linear-gradient(transparent 62%, pink 62%)` tha. Wo content-area ke 62% se neeche paint hota tha, isliye heading size par baseline ke neeche aur descenders ke upar aa jaata tha.
- **Naya `.kw`:** solid `--pink-soft` background, `display: inline`, `vertical-align: baseline`, `padding: 0.02em 0.2em`, `margin: 0 -0.02em`, `border-radius: 0.2em`. Sab `em` me hai, to body se H2 tak scale hota hai.
- **Wrap fix:** `box-decoration-break: clone`, isliye multi-line phrase ki har line ke apne rounded ends.
- **Dark variant:** `--rose-pale` ka 30% mix, text `--surface`.
- **Gradient guard:** `-webkit-text-fill-color` explicitly set.
- **Colors:** sirf `tokens.css` se. `Highlight.tsx` me badlaav nahi.
