# 10-10-2026: Admin team access (research and plan)

Question: right now one admin sees and approves every order. If that admin is not available, how can Alok Plastics let a second person handle the work, and what is the easy, production-safe way to do it?

## 1. What the admin does today

- The panel supports more than one login, but only through the server file `admin/config.php`: add another block under `'users'` with a name and a password hash made by `php admin/hash.php`.
- Every login has full access: orders, products, careers and deleting.
- There is no screen inside the panel to add, switch off or reset a person. Someone has to edit a file on the server.
- The "forgot password" link is emailed to the owner's inbox only, so a second person cannot reset their own password.
- The activity log already records who did what (sign-ins, status changes, order edits and deletes, role changes). It becomes more useful once there are several people.

## 2. Options, from simplest to most complete

| Option | Effort | What you get | Weak point |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Add a second login in `config.php` | 5 minutes | Second person can sign in | Full access for both, and every change needs a server file edit |
| B. Team page, 2 roles (Owner, Staff) | about half a day | Owner adds and switches off people in the panel; Staff cannot delete or change products | Only two levels of access |
| C. B plus Manager role, "Assigned to" on orders, WhatsApp or email alerts | 1 to 2 days | Work can be split between people; new orders alert everyone on duty | More screens to learn |
| D. C plus two-step login, per-section permissions, automatic backups | several days | Strongest for a larger team | Too much for 1 to 5 people |

Recommendation: **B now**, then the parts of C that are needed.

## 3. Recommended design (option B)

Roles:
- **Owner**: everything, plus the Team page (add, switch off, reset passwords).
- **Staff**: view and update orders, enquiries and applications; add notes. Cannot delete orders, change products or open roles, or see the Team page.

Team page (Owner only):
1. Add person: name, username, temporary password, role.
2. Switch off or on with one click. Nothing is deleted, so the person's past actions keep their name in the activity log.
3. Reset a person's password.
4. A person changes their own password after the first sign-in.

Storage: the same folder-based data the admin already uses. No database, no new software.

## 4. Later improvements (option C)

- A **Manager** role between Owner and Staff: approves quotes and edits orders, but cannot manage the team.
- **Assigned to** on each order and a "My orders" filter, so two people do not handle the same customer.
- **Alerts** on every new order to everyone on duty (WhatsApp or email), not a single inbox.
- A second Owner, so the business is never locked out when one person is away.

## 5. Safety checklist for production

- Keep at least two Owners.
- Give each person their own login; never share one.
- Switch people off the day they leave.
- Review the activity log regularly.
- Take a daily backup of the admin data folder (orders, customers, products, roles).
- Keep `'debug'` off on the live site.
- Deleting an order is permanent; use Cancel to keep a record.

## 8. Team page, email sign-in, invites: BUILT, THEN REVERTED (10-10-2026)

**Status: reverted at the owner's request.** The admin is back to the simple login (username `owner`, plain username and password, no Team or My account screens, no roles). The notes below describe what had been built, in case it is wanted again. One fix from that work was kept: resetting or changing a `config.php` user's password now really saves.

- **Sign in with email or username** (case-insensitive). Each person can save their email under **My account**.
- **Roles:** Owner (everything) and Staff (dashboard, enquiries, orders, carts, applications, customers, My account). Staff get a "No access" page for products, careers, activity log, export, deleting and the Team page; those links are also hidden from their menu and dashboard.
- **Team page (Owner only):** add a person (name, email, role), switch off or on, change role, send a new invite or password reset, add a missing email. The last active Owner cannot be switched off or demoted, and nobody can switch off or lower themselves.
- **Invites:** no public sign-up. The Owner adds a person, the system emails a one-time link valid for 48 hours (if email is not set up, the link is shown to the Owner to copy and send on WhatsApp). The person chooses their own password, then signs in with their email.
- **Forgot password:** works with email or username; the link goes to that person's own email (the owner's inbox if they have none). The answer is always the same, so nobody can find out which emails exist. Limited to 6 requests per hour per address.
- **My account:** change email and password (current password required).
- **Storage:** `admin/data/team.json` (people added on the Team screen). People in `config.php` stay Owners.
- **Fixed on the way:** resetting or changing the password of a `config.php` user reported success but did not save it. It now saves.
- **Needs the live server:** set `$ALOK_FROM_EMAIL` and `$ALOK_TO_EMAIL` in `api/config.php` so invites and resets are emailed; until then the Owner copies the link.
